At a glance

What
A personal referral link for partner university sites. The referrer gets an e-gift card after the friend enrols and an admin approves it. A fee discount for the friend (the friend benefit) ships in v3; v1 shows full fees (12.1).
Rewards
The admin sets the tiers in each campaign (4.1). The last tier decides the maximum number of gift cards. Suggested values, used in every example here: ₹1,000 for friend 1, ₹5,000 each for friends 2 to 6, ₹7,000 each for friends 7 to 11. That is at most 11 cards, or ₹61,000.

The referral system must be changed to support non-enrolled referrers. People may refer themselves using a second number (3.7).

0.1Problem

Partner university sites have no referral programme today. Students, alumni and their families already recommend these degrees. But nobody tracks it, nobody gets credit, and there is no reason to recommend more. Referred people show up as ordinary leads, or not at all.

Refer and Earn fixes this with one personal link. The link proves who referred whom. The referrer is paid only after the friend really enrols.

2.User stories

2.1Visitor (thinking about referring)

  • I want to understand in seconds what I get and what my friend gets.
  • I want to see how much I could earn.
  • I want my link quickly, without long forms or enrolling myself.
  • I want to trust this is real: stories, top referrers, clear rules.

2.2Referrer

  • I want to share my link in one tap on WhatsApp or any app.
  • I want to see where each friend is without calling the university.
  • I want to know when each gift card will arrive and how much it is.
  • I want to know how far I am from the next tier and the maximum.

2.3Friend

  • I want to know who invited me.
  • I want a discount on my fees for being referred. v3
  • I want to see my price before giving my details.
  • I want to speak to a counsellor before deciding.

2.4Business team

  • I want every reward approved by a person before it is paid, so we never pay for an enrolment that is reversed.
  • I want a maximum reward per referrer, so the cost is known in advance.
  • I want every referred lead tagged with its referrer in the CRM, so referrals can be compared with other channels.
  • I want self-referrals, duplicates and people already on the site blocked automatically.

2.5Marketing team

  • I want one referrer page that the homepage banner, footer, email and WhatsApp campaigns can all point to.
  • I want to see which of those channels brings the most referrers.
  • I want a ready-made share message so referrers spread the offer with no effort.
  • I want stories, a leaderboard and an activity ticker on the page to build trust.
  • I want amounts on the page to come from the campaign, so we never promise something we don't pay.
  • I want time-limited bonus campaigns later. v2

3.How it works

Four ideas hold the whole programme together:

  1. The link is the proof. A referral counts only if the friend signs up through the referrer's link. No codes, no manual claims.
  2. The friend sees who invited them. Opening the link shows the referrer's name. In v1 prices are full fees; the friend discount comes in v3 (12.1). If the programme is paused, the link opens as an ordinary page (4.1).
  3. Pay only after approval. The gift card is held from the day the friend pays the enrolment fee. An admin approves it (4.2), and it goes out by email and SMS. The referrer never claims anything, and nothing is taken back.
  4. One account per phone number. Students, leads and referrers all share the same kind of profile. A profile becomes a referrer the moment it gets a link (8).

3.2Who counts

  • A referrer can't refer their own phone number.
  • Each friend can have only one referrer: whoever's link they signed up through first.
  • A friend counts only if their phone number is new to the site. Anyone who already has a profile (a referrer, a lead, a past lead or a student) is not eligible.
  • No KYC or PAN is asked from referrers, however much they earn.

3.4Reward logic

⚙ Backend requirement
Gift cards run on the existing referral system, through campaigns of a new type: Non-enrolled referral flow (4.1). The campaign holds the tiers and their amounts and names. The numbers below are the suggested settings. If the admin changes them, the amounts change too (try it in 3.6). Every page reads these values from the campaign; nothing is typed in by hand. The reward is always an e-gift card from the existing voucher vendor, never cash.

Step 1: place in line

Each eligible friend who pays the enrolment fee gets a place in line. The first to enrol is place 1. Sign-up order doesn't matter. Friends who were not eligible get no place. A friend who is refunded keeps their place, and it isn't given to anyone else.

Step 2: place decides the amount

TierPlacesEachTotal so far
Starter1₹1,000₹1,000
Silver2 to 6₹5,000₹26,000
Gold7 to 11₹7,000₹61,000
Past the last range12 and up (when the last range ends at 11)No card. Status Max reached.—

Step 3: hold, wait, send

  • Friend pays the enrolment fee → the card is held at their place's amount. Status Scheduled.
  • An admin approves it in bulk (4.2) → the card is sent by email and SMS.
  • Nobody can change the amount once it is held, before or after it is sent.

Step 4: refunds

  • Refund or cancelled admission (Cancelled, Registration Declined, ARU - Audit Failed) before approval → only that card is cancelled (Void). Nobody else's place or amount changes.
  • Refund after the card is sent → nothing changes. The card is kept.
  • Because a refunded place isn't reused, it still counts towards the maximum. So a referrer with one refund can earn one card fewer than the maximum: 10 instead of 11.

3.7Fraud controls

Fake accounts don't pay off here, because every gift card needs a paid enrolment and a person's approval. The real risks are:

  • someone referring themselves with a second number
  • small groups referring each other
  • agents posting links in public to collect gift cards for students who would have enrolled anyway

4.1Setting up the campaign

Gift cards are set up as a campaign in the referral portal (Create new campaign). We add a new campaign type, Non-enrolled referral flow. A campaign of this type:

  • covers one university, across all its batches and programmes
  • adds one setting: tier names (the friend's discount follows in v3, 12.1)
  • has no separate reward cap, because the last range sets the maximum
  • has no End Date, and runs until it is replaced or switched off

Everything else in the form stays as it is. General and Sales campaigns don't change.

How the maximum works

  • Counts every gift card that is held (Scheduled) or sent. A refunded place still counts.
  • Checked when a friend pays the enrolment fee, so two friends enrolling at once can't both take the last place.
  • Which maximum applies. The maximum of the campaign that was live when the friend signed up. For example, campaign A ends at 11 and campaign B, which replaced it, ends at 8. A referrer already has 9 cards. A friend who signed up under B gets no card, because 9 is past B's maximum. A friend who signed up under A still gets one, at place 10.
  • Once reached, no gift card is held. The friend's status is Max reached, and it is final: it never turns into a gift card later, because refunded places are not reused. The dashboard shows "No gift card (maximum reached)".
  • The dashboard's "3 of 11 gift cards" line and progress bar use the live campaign's maximum. If the programme is paused, they use the last campaign's.

How tier names work

  • Referrers see the name typed for each range. It appears on the dashboard's gift card levels, its "Done" and "You're here" tags, and the landing page.
  • Ranges with the same name show as one tier. So 7–9 and 10–11, both named Gold, appear as "Gold · Friends 7 to 11".

No end date: a campaign runs until it is replaced or deactivated

A Non-enrolled referral flow campaign has a Start Date but no End Date. It runs until an admin switches it off or a new campaign replaces it.

Rules for the Non-enrolled referral flow type

These apply only to this campaign type. General and Sales campaigns work as they do today.

  • One count per referrer. A referrer's place in line carries over from one campaign to the next at the same university.
  • Sign-up decides the terms. A friend's referral uses the amounts and maximum of the campaign that was live when they signed up. This holds even if that campaign is later replaced or switched off.
  • A live campaign is locked. Before its Start Date, everything can be edited. After it, only the Campaign Name can change. To change the tiers, the admin schedules a new campaign, which replaces this one on its Start Date.
  • Referrer Type comes from the referrer's lead. A referrer is Enrolled if their Lead Admission Status is Confirmed (fee paid); otherwise Non-enrolled. It is checked when the friend's lead is created. Non-enrolled referrers use this campaign type; Enrolled referrers stay on the existing campaign, as today.
  • Referrers who enrol mid-way. Once a referrer's admission is Confirmed, their later friends go to the existing campaign. Count and maximum from this campaign type don't carry over. Accepted.

12.1Friend benefit (v3)

Not in v1, v1.1 or v2. Until v3, friends pay the full fee, and no page, message or system shows or applies a discount. This section holds everything the discount needs, so v3 can be built from it alone.

The rule

  • A friend who signs up through a referral link gets a discount on the programme fee. The admin sets it per campaign: default 10%, 1% to 100%.
  • The same discount applies to every programme in the campaign, so a heading like "Get 10% off" is true everywhere.
  • The discount comes from the campaign live when the friend signed up. It is locked to the friend's phone number and never expires, even for a later batch.
  • It applies to whichever programme at this university the friend finally enrols in, not only the one they picked on the link page.
  • Max reached and flagged referrals still get the discount. The referrer's cap and fraud flags never affect it.

Who doesn't get it

  • Anyone whose number already has a profile on the site. They see: "This number is already registered with us, so the referral discount doesn't apply. A counsellor will call you."
  • Anyone who signs up while the programme is paused, or through an invalid link.
  • Friends who signed up before v3 went live. Sign-up time decides.
